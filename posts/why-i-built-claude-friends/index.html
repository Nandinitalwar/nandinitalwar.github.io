Why I Built Claude-Friends

June 4, 2026

Making the terminal feel more educational.

An overarching theme I noticed at Claude Code for Women in AI was that despite the “intimidating” feel of the terminal, people preferred it because it made them feel like a “real coder.” I resonated with that - when I first discovered Claude Code, I coded exclusively, and insufferably to my friends, in Vim that semester. It felt simpler, faster, more nimble than VS Code, but more importantly, cooler.

All of this made me wonder whether there was a way to make Claude Code feel less daunting - more inviting, more personalizable. So I built a statusline called claude-friends.

It shows you the repository, branch, token usage, and usage remaining, alongside two features I thought would make the experience more human: a daily streak calculator, and a feature that lets you see which of your friends are online.

The streak was inspired by Snapchat - a small dopamine hit that rewards consistency. The friends-online feature was inspired by the feeling I had walking into that meetup: knowing other people are building at the same time as you makes the whole thing feel less solitary.

I wanted the terminal to feel like a place, not just a tool.

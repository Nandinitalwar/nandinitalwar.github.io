Education Research

Less Polished, More Learned: Model Routing and Lower-Token Outputs as a Pedagogical Lever in STEM Independent Research, Education & AI · 2026 · In preparation for FIE 2027, ICCE 2027, and IEEE TALE 2027
  • Argues that routing STEM learners to smaller models and deliberately terser, lower-token outputs can improve learning — the polish and completeness of frontier-model answers often short-circuits the productive struggle that builds durable understanding
  • Grounds the claim in learning science: desirable difficulties, the generation effect, and productive failure all show that students learn more when an answer is scaffolded or left incomplete and they have to close the gap themselves
  • Proposes an adaptive routing policy that trades output completeness for engagement — hints and partial reasoning over full worked solutions, cheaper models for practice — with the secondary benefit of far lower token cost at classroom scale
Restructuring STEM Curricula in South Mumbai's Low-Literacy Municipal Schools for an AI-Native Generation Independent Research, Education & AI · 2026 · In preparation for FIE 2027, ICCE 2027, and IEEE TALE 2027
  • Argues that in teacher-scarce, low-resource classrooms, conversational AI tutors can partially close the instruction gap by giving each student adaptive, one-on-one explanation in their own language and at their own pace
  • Confronts the equity and infrastructure risks head-on — device access, connectivity, data privacy, and the danger of widening the digital divide — and outlines a phased, low-bandwidth rollout designed for the realities of BMC schools in Dadar, Mahim, and Colaba
How Can Agastya's Programs Be Effectively Implemented in Municipal Schools? Independent Research, Developmental Economics · Oct 2017 – Mar 2018 · paper
  • Authored and published independent developmental economics research on effectively implementing Agastya International Foundation's hands-on STEM teacher training programs in Mumbai's lowest-literacy municipal schools run by the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC)
  • Presented at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur and FLAME University, Pune, to a panel of ~20 professors and students; invited to present at the University of International Business and Economics, Beijing
  • Analyzed the funding hurdles facing BMC schools and how Agastya's participatory, government-syllabus-aligned model — already scaled through state partnerships in Karnataka, Bihar, and Punjab — can raise education quality and future workforce productivity

AI in Education

Applied projects exploring how conversational AI can teach, explain, and transfer expert knowledge to everyday users

Pinch Conversational AI Tutor · Oct 2025 – Present · code
  • Solo-built and shipped a full-stack conversational AI product across Twilio SMS and WhatsApp Business, reaching 200 weekly active users, that acts as a personalized tutor — teaching users their birth charts and astrological concepts through back-and-forth dialogue rather than static readouts
  • A case study in AI-driven education: how a large language model can adapt explanations to each learner, sustain engagement across sessions, and make an unfamiliar knowledge domain approachable. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Gemini Flash, PlanetScale, Convex, Parallel API, and Vercel
Skinopsis Clinic Mumbai AI Knowledge-Capture System · Feb 2026 – Present · code · clinic
  • Built an AI audio-to-text system for Skinopsis Clinic Mumbai (a dermatology practice under Dr. Amrita Talwar and Dr. Sunita Tagra, serving 50 patients daily) that captures doctors' spoken expertise and structures it into clear, patient-facing prescriptions
  • An exploration of AI as a knowledge-transfer tool: cutting documentation from ~60 minutes to ~5 minutes a day (a 90% reduction) so clinicians can spend more time educating patients on their care. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Python FastAPI
